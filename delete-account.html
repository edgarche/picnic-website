Delete Your Picnic Account

Picnic, developed by Picnic, gives you full control over your account and your data. Here is how to request deletion, and exactly what happens to your information when you do.

Step by Step

How to Request Deletion

You can delete your account directly from the Picnic app in under a minute.

  1. Open the Picnic app and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Profile in the bottom navigation.
  3. Tap Account Settings.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion when prompted. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Don't have access to the app?

Email support@startapicnic.com from the address associated with your account and request account deletion. We will confirm your identity and process the request within 30 days.

What Happens to Your Data

Data Deleted vs. Retained

When you delete your account, most of your personal data is permanently removed. A limited amount is retained as described below.

🗑️

Deleted within 30 days

  • Your profile, photos, and account details
  • Your login credentials
  • Direct messages and group chat history
  • Saved preferences and settings
📁

Retained after deletion

  • Anonymized or aggregated data used for analytics and product improvement, which no longer identifies you
  • Picnic or event history you were part of, retained in de-identified form so the record stays accurate for other attendees
  • Information we are required to keep for legal, safety, or regulatory reasons

For the complete data retention policy, see Section 4 of our Privacy Policy.
